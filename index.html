About

I'm Chad Muirhead, a Jamaican-born iOS engineer based in Bloomfield, Connecticut. I started Di Nerd Apps in 2019 to build the apps I wished existed: tools that make city data easy to use, and apps that celebrate Caribbean culture.

I also teach SwiftUI on YouTube and spoke at CTData 2026 on building your own apps and databases with AI.
